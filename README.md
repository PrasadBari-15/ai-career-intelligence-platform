## 📌 About

**CareerIQ** is a full-stack career intelligence platform that helps users understand their resume, compare it with job descriptions, identify missing skills, and create a career roadmap.

The goal is to bring important career analysis features into one simple platform.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **User Authentication** — Registration, login and JWT authentication
- 📄 **Resume Intelligence** — Upload and analyze PDF resumes
- 🧠 **Resume Analysis** — Detect skills, education, experience and projects
- 🎯 **Job Matching** — Compare resumes with job descriptions
- 🟢 **Matching Skills** — Identify skills already present in the resume
- 🟠 **Missing Skills** — Identify skills required by the job
- 🧩 **Skill Gap** — Understand areas that need improvement
- 🗺️ **Career Roadmap** — Get structured next steps
- 📊 **Dashboard** — View important career metrics in one place
